# 🧪 Chemistry Lab Portfolio

**Adrien Jefferson | Academic coursework collection**

A collection of chemistry laboratory worksheets and quantitative exercises documenting measurement, chemical calculations, observations, and scientific reporting.

## Explore the collection

| Entry | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Measurement and Density](measurement/README.md) | Measuring liquid volumes; comparing metal density by geometry and water displacement; solution density using buret measurements; glassware selection and measurement precision. |
| [Density and Data Analysis](data-lab/README.md) | Investigating penny density using water displacement and the density of an unknown liquid; practicing data tables, averages, graphing concepts, and error analysis. |
| [Experimental Determination of the Gas Constant](ideal-gas-law/README.md) | Collecting hydrogen from magnesium and hydrochloric acid in two trials; applying stoichiometry, water-vapor pressure corrections, unit conversions, and PV = nRT. |
| [Empirical Formula of a Metal Oxide](metal-oxide/README.md) | Using mass changes during magnesium oxidation to determine a metal-to-oxygen mole ratio and empirical formula; documenting observations and considering experimental error. |
| [Significant Figures and Dimensional Analysis](significant-figures/README.md) | Practicing scientific notation, rounding, significant figures, and unit conversions through calculation exercises, including a fictional Mars resource-planning scenario. |
| [Acid-Base Titration](titration/README.md) | Standardizing sodium hydroxide using potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP); determining hydrochloric acid concentration across three trials; applying dilution and neutralization calculations. |

## About these materials

Original images and the available measurement PDF are preserved. Some worksheets include instructor feedback, calculation errors, or incomplete responses. Inclusion documents coursework and learning; it does not imply that every answer is correct. No new experiments or completed reanalyses are claimed.

Portfolio summaries were prepared later with AI assistance using the supplied coursework. Worksheet prompts and instructional material remain attributable to their original course authors. No reuse license is granted for those materials.
